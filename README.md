# Research

This repository is intended to store and share my research, studies, ideas, and discoveries on topics that interest me.

I created this space to organize everything I learn over time, document my research, and track how my knowledge evolves.

I don’t intend to limit myself to a single area. I enjoy exploring different topics, understanding how things work, and seeking answers to questions.

## What will you find here?

- **Research:** in-depth investigations into specific topics.
- **Studies:** notes, explanations, and content I’m learning.
- **Experiments:** tests, observations, and attempts to put ideas into practice.
- **Reflections:** thoughts, hypotheses, and questions that arise during my studies.

## How do I organize my research?

Each research project will have its own section, where I plan to document what I’m investigating, why I chose that topic, what sources I used, and what I’ve discovered.

Not everything here will necessarily be a completed research project. Some things may just be initial ideas, ongoing studies, or questions I’m still trying to answer.

## Why does this repository exist?

Because I enjoy learning, and I believe that documenting what I discover is a good way to better understand these topics.

I also want to be able to revisit old research, see how my ideas have changed, and—who knows—help others who are curious about the same topics.

## A Note

Everything here is part of my learning process. I may make mistakes, change my mind, or find information that contradicts my previous conclusions.

Therefore, these posts may be revised and updated as I discover new things.
